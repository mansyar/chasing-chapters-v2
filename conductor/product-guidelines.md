# Product Guidelines

## Brand & Voice
- **Feel:** warm, literary, intimate — "boutique bookstore meets personal journal", never corporate or loud
- **Voice:** thoughtful, personal, first-person reviewer perspective; Indonesian translations keep the same warmth

## Visual Identity
- Warm cream palette (oklch), Playfair Display (serif headings) + Inter (body)
- Book/reading motifs; covers treated as visual heroes; blur placeholders for images
- Full dark mode support; subtle CSS animations (no heavy animation libs)

## UX Principles
- **Content-first:** reviews and covers are the stars; chrome stays minimal
- **Fast perceived performance:** skeleton loaders, ISR, optimistic UI (likes)
- **Responsive & accessible:** mobile sheet nav, focus states, sr-only labels, skip-to-content
- **Reader-friendly typography:** generous line-height, comfortable measure, readable serif headings
- **Bilingual parity:** EN and ID readers get equally complete experiences
